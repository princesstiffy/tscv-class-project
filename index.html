About the Viking

After a long day at work you sit down to play your favorite visual novel about the thrilling world of vikings. Until... you get sucked in? You now find yourself off the coast of a Nordic seaside town where you must navigate a new city, culture, friends, and your way back home.

The Team

TSCV is a small team based in Orlando, FL. We focus on building immersive visual novels for everyone to enjoy! Thank you to Sophia, Victoria, CT, Brooke, and Tiffany for coordinating and producing The Super Cool Viking together,
